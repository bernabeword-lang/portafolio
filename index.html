About the shop

Three chairs. No rush. Every fade checked twice.

Fade Line opened on Miller Ave with one rule: every cut gets checked in the mirror before you do. We keep the schedule loose enough that walk-ins rarely wait more than a coffee's worth of time.

Clippers are cleaned between every client, blades are swapped weekly, and if a line's not sharp, we fix it — no charge, no argument.
